      What is simulated

      Every one of the 104 cells runs its own single-particle electrochemical model: lithium diffusion in 20 radial shells per electrode, Butler–Volmer kinetics and measured open-circuit potentials, plus a lumped ohmic and electrolyte-polarisation term fitted to a full Doyle–Fuller–Newman model. Cells differ slightly in capacity and resistance, share current inside each parallel group, and heat through irreversible and entropic terms.

      Each cell has a core and a surface temperature. Surfaces exchange heat with their neighbours by conduction and, when hot, radiation, and with the enclosure air, which couples to the aluminium housing and to ambient through speed-dependent convection. Above 60 °C the four decomposition reactions of the Kim et al. abuse model run in each cell; the vent opens at 130 °C, the separator fails at 170 °C and dumps the remaining charge through an internal short.

      The scooter is a longitudinal-dynamics model: inertia, rolling resistance, aerodynamic drag and road grade, with a drive-efficiency map, a 3.2 kW motor limit and BMS-limited regenerative braking.

      Where the numbers come from

      • Cell parameters: Chen et al. (2020), J. Electrochem. Soc. 167, 080534, LG M50 21700, via the PyBaMM parameter set.
      • Entropic coefficients and diffusion activation energies: O'Regan et al. (2022), Electrochim. Acta 425, 140700.
      • Abuse kinetics: Kim, Pesaran and Spotnitz (2007), J. Power Sources 170, 476. Fitted for LiCoO₂ and graphite, so applied to this NMC cell only as an approximation.
      • Speed traces: WMTC Part 1 and Part 2 (UN GTR No. 2, the two-wheeler cycle used for Indian type approval since BS IV) and a real recorded trip with road grade from NREL's Transportation Secure Data Center, both as shipped with NREL FASTSim.
      • Assumed and representative: vehicle mass and drag, drive efficiency, thermal network conductances, gas yields, enclosure volume, and sensor error figures typical of each sensor class. These are editable estimates, not measurements.

      Limits

      The single-particle model loses accuracy above about 1.5C; the 2C test shows it. Ageing, lithium plating, overcharge chemistry, flame and ejecta impingement are not modelled, so propagation between cells may be underestimated. Treat thermal runaway timing as qualitative.
